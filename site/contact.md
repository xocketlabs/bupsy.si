## What to write to us about

- **Early access.** If you'd like to try Bupsy before your waitlist spot opens, tell us what you'd use it for. We let people in a few at a time.
- **Questions about Bupsy.** How it works with Claude Code or Codex, what your bots can and can't do, how approvals and routines work.
- **Your data.** To remove your address from the waitlist, or to ask what we hold about you. The answer is short: see [Privacy](https://bupsy.pages.dev/privacy).
- **Press and partnerships.** Writing about Bupsy, or building something that works with it.
- **Security.** If you've found a problem, write here with the details, and please give us time to fix it before making it public.

## Other ways to learn about Bupsy

- [About](https://bupsy.pages.dev/about): why Bupsy exists and who makes it
- [Developers](https://bupsy.pages.dev/developers): the public MCP server, the waitlist API and Markdown for agents
- [Join the waitlist](https://bupsy.pages.dev/#join): one email when your spot opens

Bupsy is not affiliated with Anthropic or OpenAI. For help with Claude Code or Codex themselves, contact their makers.
