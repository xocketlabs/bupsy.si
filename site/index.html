Routines and goals

"Brief me every weekday at 7." "Keep my inbox under a day old." It checks on a schedule and acts when needed.

Questions with answers

Connect any tool

See what it changed

Every bot a character

Eight shapes, eight colours. Faces that look around, bounce while they work and wave when they need you.

Long chats stay cheap

One click, or automatically: the bot writes itself notes and carries on in a fresh chat, so each message costs a fraction.

Your commands come too
